## Samsung Health: consent to AI training or lose your health data <a id="samsung-health-ai-consent"></a>

**Source:** [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/13/samsung-health-ai-training-data-consent/) · **Type:** policy change · **Time (UTC):** ~08:00 July 13

As part of Samsung Health's redesign rollout, Samsung is sending consent prompts to existing users requesting permission to use their health data for "AI training and modelling, including human review." Data covered includes step counts, sleep patterns, medication logs, cycle tracking, and full health records including treatments and test results. Users who decline or later withdraw consent are told that the app will stop syncing health data to their Samsung account and will permanently delete their stored data once any legally mandated retention period expires. Opt-out settings are available under Samsung Health → Settings → Privacy → "Consent to the use of health data for AI training and modelling." The story became the top HN post on July 14.

**Why it matters:** This is a significant precedent: a major health app making core functionality contingent on AI training consent. Unlike typical data-use notices, the consequence of refusal isn't degraded features — it's data deletion. Engineers building health applications should note this as a case study in consent architecture that is likely to attract regulatory scrutiny under GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and upcoming AI data-use laws.
