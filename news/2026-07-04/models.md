## Grok 4.5 Enters Private Beta at SpaceX and Tesla <a id="grok-45-private-beta"></a>

**Source:** [xAI / Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/musk-says-grok-45-enters-private-beta-at-spacex-tesla-1840769e) · **Type:** release (private beta) · **Time (UTC):** Jun 28, ~15:00

Elon Musk announced on June 28 that Grok 4.5 has entered private beta inside SpaceX and Tesla engineering teams, making it the first deployment of the model ahead of any public rollout. The model is built on xAI's V9 foundation (completed May 26), carries roughly 1.5 trillion parameters — approximately three times the size of the V8-small architecture used in earlier Grok 4 variants — and was post-trained on synthetic code and reasoning data sourced from the Cursor platform. xAI's internal evaluations place its performance "close to, and perhaps exceeding," Claude Opus, but no independent benchmarks or system card have been published. The company has signalled a monthly from-scratch training cadence through year-end, with Grok 5 targeting 6–10 trillion parameters.

**Why it matters:** The private-dogfood deployment inside two engineering-heavy Musk companies is a deliberate capability signal targeting enterprise technical users, not consumer chat. No independent benchmark data exist yet, so performance claims remain unverified — but if xAI maintains its monthly cadence, a public Grok 4.5 release is likely within weeks and would re-enter the frontier model comparison cycle.
