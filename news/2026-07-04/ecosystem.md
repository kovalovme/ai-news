## Pentagon–Anthropic Weapons Dispute: Court Emails Show the Real Fight <a id="pentagon-anthropic-weapons"></a>

**Source:** [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/read-the-tense-emails-between-the-pentagon-former-uber-exec-and-anthropic-dario-amodei-2000780849) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-pentagon-emails-amodei-michael-guardrails) · **Type:** policy/legal · **Time (UTC):** Jul 03

Court documents unsealed July 3 and reported by Gizmodo and WSJ reveal months of email exchanges between Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Pentagon Undersecretary Emil Michael (the former Uber exec confirmed as Defense Undersecretary in early 2026). Michael sought access to Claude for "all lawful national-security use cases" without carve-outs, while Amodei maintained two hard limits: fully autonomous weapon systems and domestic mass surveillance. Michael found the guardrails "just not workable" and gave Anthropic "one more chance to align on core principles." Talks broke down, leading to President Trump directing federal agencies to cease Anthropic use (February 27), the DoD classifying Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, and a preliminary injunction in Anthropic's favor (later reversed by an appeals court in April). A Congressional Research Service report on the dispute was published as a policy brief for Congress.

**Why it matters:** The leaked email chain is the most concrete public record to date of how a frontier AI lab's safety red lines collide with government procurement requirements. The dispute is not resolved: federal litigation is ongoing, and the policy precedent it sets — whether autonomous weapons constitute a legitimate exclusion category from government AI contracts — has implications for every lab that sells models to US defense agencies.

## OpenAI Proposes 5% US Government Equity Stake Modeled on Alaska Permanent Fund <a id="openai-government-stake"></a>

**Source:** [Financial Times via Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/07/02/openai-reportedly-pitches-granting-us-government-5-stake/) · **Type:** policy · **Time (UTC):** Jul 02

The Financial Times reported July 2 that OpenAI has been discussing offering the US government a 5% equity stake, currently worth approximately $42.6 billion based on OpenAI's ~$852 billion private valuation. The proposal, which Sam Altman has raised with President Trump, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, is structured as a voluntary framework encouraging all leading US AI labs to contribute similar stakes to a government-backed vehicle modeled after the Alaska Permanent Fund — which invests state oil revenues and distributes annual dividends to residents. Congressional approval would be required, and it is unclear whether other labs have been approached. The proposal comes days after OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol general availability was delayed pending government review.

**Why it matters:** The proposal is as much a political risk-management instrument as an economic one. By offering the government a financial stake in AI upside, OpenAI would align executive branch incentives with its commercial success — complicating the kind of punitive supply-chain designation applied to Anthropic. Whether the structure would survive Congressional scrutiny, or whether other labs such as Anthropic and Google would accept the same terms, remains unclear.

## Together AI Raises $800M Series C at $8.3B Valuation <a id="together-ai-800m"></a>

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/neocloud-together-ai-raises-800m-leaps-to-8-3b-valuation/) · **Type:** funding · **Time (UTC):** Jul 01

Together AI closed an $800 million Series C on July 1, led by Aramco Ventures (the venture arm of Saudi Aramco), with participation from NVIDIA, Vista Equity Partners, General Catalyst, Emergence Capital, Salesforce Ventures, and SentinelOne's S Ventures. The round brings total funding to $1.3 billion and values the company at $8.3 billion. Together offers an open-source inference platform that lets enterprises run DeepSeek, LongCat-2.0, Llama 4, and other open-weight models at substantially lower cost than closed APIs, and plans to grow computing capacity roughly 50-fold over the next five years. The Aramco involvement mirrors a broader pattern of Gulf sovereign capital flowing into AI infrastructure in 2026.

**Why it matters:** Aramco-led investment into an open-source inference neocloud is a direct strategic bet against closed model dependency. It also signals that the "closed model stall" narrative — frontier models delayed or restricted through H1 2026 — is driving capital toward the open-source stack as enterprise insurance. NVIDIA's participation secures GPU supply priority; Aramco's brings data-center power and cooling capacity in a region with abundant energy.

## UN "AI for Good" Global Commission Launched: Tech CEOs and World Leaders <a id="un-ai-for-good-commission"></a>

**Source:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/01/un-ai-commission-ceos-world-leaders) · [Eastern Herald](https://easternherald.com/2026/07/02/un-ai-governance-commission-jensen-huang-jassy-benioff-geneva/) · **Type:** policy · **Time (UTC):** Jul 01

The United Nations and the International Telecommunication Union launched the "AI for Good Global Commission" on July 1 — the first UN-level governance body to include the CEOs of the companies building the world's most powerful AI systems. Marc Benioff (Salesforce CEO) and Rwandan President Paul Kagame serve as co-chairs; technical members include Jensen Huang (NVIDIA), Andy Jassy (Amazon), Brad Smith (Microsoft), Jack Clark (Anthropic co-founder), Aidan Gomez (Cohere co-founder), and Mukesh Ambani. The commission's inaugural meeting is July 8 in Geneva, coinciding with the first UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance (July 6–7) and the ITU AI for Good Global Summit (July 7–10), which is expected to draw 11,000+ participants from 169 countries.

**Why it matters:** Previous multilateral AI governance efforts (e.g., G7 Hiroshima Process, OECD AI Principles) were government-only bodies. Including lab CEOs and chip company executives as commission members is structurally different: it creates a formal advisory channel between industry and UN member states, but also raises questions about regulatory capture that critics have already flagged. The first substantive output — whether the commission produces binding recommendations or voluntary frameworks — will determine its real significance.

## Crunchbase H1 2026: Global VC Hits $510B, OpenAI and Anthropic Take 43% <a id="crunchbase-h1-2026"></a>

**Source:** [Crunchbase / Tom's Hardware reporting](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/openai-floats-5-percent-government-stake-days-after-washington-delayed-gpt-5-6) · **Type:** data · **Time (UTC):** Jul 03

Crunchbase's H1 2026 venture capital report shows global VC hit a record $510 billion in the first half of the year. OpenAI and Anthropic together captured $217 billion — 43% of total global VC — reflecting the concentration of capital at the frontier model layer. This follows Anthropic's $65B Series H (valuation $965B) and OpenAI's multiple mega-rounds ahead of its confidential IPO S-1 (filed June 8). The two-lab concentration is the highest single-category VC share ever recorded in Crunchbase data.

**Why it matters:** Two companies absorbing 43% of all global venture capital in a six-month period is a structural anomaly that raises questions about capital efficiency and whether downstream AI infrastructure, application, and tooling companies are being crowded out or simply outcompeted. The figure also contextualizes the Together AI raise: the remaining 57% of $510B still represents a record level of non-frontier AI investment, and infrastructure neoclouds are a primary beneficiary.

## DeepSeek Legacy API Endpoints Retire July 24 — Developer Action Required <a id="deepseek-api-deprecation"></a>

**Source:** [DeepSeek API Docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260424) · **Type:** update · **Time (UTC):** ongoing

DeepSeek will retire the `deepseek-chat` and `deepseek-reasoner` model name aliases at 15:59 UTC on July 24, 2026. After that date, requests using either legacy name will fail entirely. During the grace period, both names are transparently routed to `deepseek-v4-flash`. Migration path: replace `deepseek-chat` with `deepseek-v4-flash` and `deepseek-reasoner` with `deepseek-v4-pro` in all routing configs, SDK integrations, and infrastructure-as-code. DeepSeek has also launched a dedicated Anthropic API format endpoint at `https://api.deepseek.com/anthropic` for Claude Code and other Anthropic-native clients.

**Why it matters:** This is a namespace change, not a model sunset — the underlying V4 Flash engine continues running. But any gateway config, LangChain integration, cost alert, or monitoring rule tied to the legacy string will silently break after July 24. With 20 days remaining, teams using DeepSeek in production should audit their model name references now.
