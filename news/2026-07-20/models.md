# Models — 2026-07-20

## Qwen 3.8-Max: Alibaba's 2.4T multimodal preview <a id="qwen-38-max"></a>

**Source:** [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/alibabas-qwen-takes-on-kimi-k3-with-open-weight-qwen-3-8-says-model-is-second-only-to-fable-5/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-19/alibaba-s-qwen-unveils-preview-of-flagship-ai-model) · **Type:** release (preview) · **Time (UTC):** Jul 19 ~12:00

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen 3.8-Max in preview on July 19, two days after Moonshot's Kimi K3 dominated Hacker News at 873 points. The model carries 2.4 trillion parameters and is Alibaba's first multimodal flagship above 1 trillion parameters, supporting text, images, video, and document inputs natively. It is accessible at 10% of standard pricing through Alibaba's Token Plan, Qoder, and QoderWork. The team states it ranks "second only to Fable 5" — but has published internal evaluations only; no third-party or LMSYS benchmark results are available at launch. An open-weight release is described as coming "soon," which would break Alibaba's pattern of keeping its Max-tier models closed-source.

**Why it matters:** If the open-weight promise holds, a 2.4T multimodal model entering the open ecosystem shortly after Kimi K3 (2.8T) would give developers and inference providers two frontier-scale Chinese open models within weeks. The absence of independent benchmarks means engineering teams should treat performance claims cautiously until ArtificialAnalysis or LMSYS scores are available.
