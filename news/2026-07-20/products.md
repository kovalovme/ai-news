# Products — 2026-07-20

## Fable 5 permanent access goes live in Max and Team Premium <a id="fable-5-live"></a>

**Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com) · **Type:** product update · **Time (UTC):** Jul 20 00:00

As announced July 18, Anthropic's Fable 5 is now included at 50% of rate limits in all Max and Team Premium subscription plans starting today, ending six weeks of rolling promotional extensions. Pro subscribers receive a $100 usage credit for metered Fable 5 access. (The July 19 digest covered the announcement; this entry marks activation day.)

**Why it matters:** Teams that have been deferring Fable 5 adoption pending pricing clarity now have a stable reference point. The 50% rate-limit inclusion changes the marginal-cost calculation for reasoning and agentic workloads on Max or Team Premium — usage below that ceiling is effectively subscription-included; usage above it reverts to $10/$50 per MTok metered billing.
