## FLI AI Safety Index Summer 2026: No lab scores above C+; military pivot flagged <a id="fli-ai-safety-index-summer-2026"></a>

**Source:** [Future of Life Institute](https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/) · [Time](https://time.com/article/2026/07/07/ai-safety-rankings-openai-anthropic-meta/) · [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4611964-google-anthropic-openai-lead-ai-safety-index-spacexai-receives-f) · **Type:** report · **Time (UTC):** — (published July 7)

The Future of Life Institute's Summer 2026 AI Safety Index evaluated nine AI companies across six safety domains using evidence collected through June 3, 2026, reviewed by an independent panel of seven researchers and governance experts. Anthropic leads at C+ (2.66/5), followed by OpenAI at C (2.28), Google DeepMind at C (2.01), Meta at D+ (1.32), Z.ai at D- (0.88), Alibaba Cloud at D- (0.87), xAI/SpaceXAI at F (0.65), DeepSeek at F (0.47), and Mistral at F (0.33). No company received better than a C+ in any single domain; all companies received failing grades in existential safety.

The panel's headline finding: companies including Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Meta—which previously barred military applications—have reversed course and are actively pursuing defense partnerships. The panel classified this as "an emerging current harm risk." Three companies received F grades spanning three regions (U.S.: xAI/SpaceXAI; China: DeepSeek; Europe: Mistral), which the report notes rules out geopolitical partisanship as an explanatory variable.

**Why it matters:** The index is the most comprehensive public comparative assessment available, and its finding that companies are weakening prior safety commitments (rather than strengthening them) directly contradicts public messaging from several labs. Engineers and procurement teams evaluating model providers now have a structured, third-party baseline that includes capability-level competitors from China and Europe.
