# Products — 2026-07-19

## Fable 5 promotional access expires; Max/Team Premium transition takes effect <a id="fable5-transition"></a>

**Source:** [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/18/claude-make-fable-5-permanent/) · [Anthropic newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) · **Type:** update · **Time (UTC):** 06:59 (midnight PT)

The six-week promotional window for Claude Fable 5 closes at 23:59 PT July 19 (06:59 UTC July 20). Starting July 20, Fable 5 becomes a permanent feature in Max and Team Premium plans at 50% of standard weekly limits. Pro and Team Standard users receive a one-time $100 usage credit; subsequent Fable 5 access reverts to API pricing ($10/M input, $50/M output). The 50% Claude Code weekly rate-limit boost — running concurrently — also ends at the same moment.

**Why it matters:** This concludes the saga that began with Fable 5's launch and its two promotional extensions. Max and Team Premium subscribers retain access permanently (at half limits), while Pro remains a metered-credit tier for frontier use. Engineers on Pro who relied on Fable 5 daily for code review or long-context tasks should audit their monthly token spend now.
