## EU AI Act transparency code: signatory deadline July 22, rules live August 2 <a id="eu-ai-act-transparency-code"></a>

**Source:** [EC Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content) · [TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/the-eus-ai-transparency-code-of-practice-explained/) · [Bird & Bird analysis](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/taking-the-eu-ai-act-to-practice-the-final-transparency-code-of-practice) · **Type:** regulatory deadline · **Time (UTC):** —

On July 8, the European Commission concluded that the Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content adequately covers the obligations under Articles 50(2), (4), and (5) of the EU AI Act. Organizations wishing to sign as signatories must submit their forms by 18:00 CET on July 22, 2026; signatories will be publicly listed ahead of the August 2 compliance deadline, when the AI Act's Article 50 marking and labelling obligations for AI-generated content come into force. The code is split into two separate sections — one for model providers, one for deployers — which can be signed independently. It does not mandate a single technical approach; instead it requires layered solutions that combine metadata, watermarking, and content-provenance mechanisms meeting four criteria: effectiveness, interoperability, robustness, and reliability. Companies that do not sign may still comply through other means but will face higher enforcement scrutiny, with less predictable legal exposure.

**Why it matters:** August 2 is the first hard enforcement date under the EU AI Act for any widely deployed AI capability. Any product that generates synthetic text, images, audio, or video for end-users in the EU is in scope; the six-day window to sign is effectively now.
