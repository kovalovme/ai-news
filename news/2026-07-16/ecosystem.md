# Ecosystem — 2026-07-16

## EU Cloud and AI Development Act published in Official Journal <a id="eu-cada"></a>

**Source:** [EC Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cloud-and-ai-development-act) · [Lawfare](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-eu-cloud-and-ai-development-act) · [Hunton & Williams](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/european-commission-advances-new-proposal-to-expand-cloud-capacity-and-ai-infrastructure) · **Type:** regulation · **Time (UTC):** Jul 15

The Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA) was published in the EU Official Journal on July 15, 2026, and takes effect on August 4. The regulation — adopted by the European Commission on June 3 as part of its Tech Sovereignty Package — targets tripling EU data-center capacity within five to seven years. The core mechanism is a four-tier cloud sovereignty framework for public-sector bodies: the tiers define escalating assurance requirements for cloud and AI services based on the sensitivity of the data processed, and public administrations must select a tier based on their own risk assessment. The first tier of requirements applies from February 2028; the highest tier becomes mandatory by August 2029. The regulation also opens a public consultation through August 27, 2026.

**Why it matters:** CADA represents the EU's most direct intervention in AI infrastructure to date — explicitly aimed at reducing reliance on non-EU cloud providers for public-sector AI workloads. For cloud providers, it creates a new procurement criterion that will shape how governments across 27 member states select AI infrastructure over the next three years.

## White House launches Gold Eagle AI cybersecurity clearinghouse <a id="gold-eagle"></a>

**Source:** [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/white-house-launches-gold-eagle-initiative-for-unprecedented-cybersecurity-vulnerability-coordination/) · [The Record](https://therecord.media/gold-eagle-cybersecurity-vulnerabilities-clearinghouse) · [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/14/tech/ai-cybersecurity-clearing-house-white-house) · **Type:** policy/launch · **Time (UTC):** ~16:00 Jul 14

The White House launched Gold Eagle on July 14, a joint Treasury/DHS/Pentagon platform that uses AI to consolidate, triage, and coordinate software vulnerability disclosures across government and critical infrastructure. The clearinghouse accepts reports from AI and cybersecurity companies, utilities, banks, and other critical infrastructure operators; AI tools automatically assess whether reported flaws are genuine, prioritize by severity, and coordinate remediation across sectors. Gold Eagle was mandated by President Trump's June 2 Executive Order 14409, "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security." The administration says it has already begun processing vulnerability reports.

**Why it matters:** The US now has a centralized AI-assisted vulnerability coordination mechanism alongside the traditional CVE/NVD system. For security teams in critical infrastructure sectors, participation is voluntary but explicitly expected; Gold Eagle creates a new channel for responsible disclosure that may become de facto standard in regulated sectors.
