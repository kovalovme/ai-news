# Products — 2026-07-05

## Google Daily Brief and Personal Intelligence roll out to US subscribers <a id="google-daily-brief-personal-intelligence"></a>

**Source:** [Google Gemini release notes](https://gemini.google/release-notes/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-gemini-app-daily-brief-redesign-io-2026) · [TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/google-s-personal-intelligence-rolls-out-to-all-us-users) · **Type:** launch · **Time (UTC):** July 2026 rollout

Two features announced at Google I/O 2026 in May are now rolling out to US users. **Daily Brief** delivers a personalized daily overview synthesized from Gmail, Google Calendar, and prior Gemini conversations, organized into "Top of Mind" items and "Look Ahead" longer-horizon tasks. **Personal Intelligence** enables Gemini to connect to a user's app ecosystem for context-aware answers. Both require a paid Google AI subscription (Plus, Pro, or Ultra) and are limited to US users 18 and older in this initial wave; UK, Canada, Australia, and India are expected next.

**Why it matters:** These are Google's most direct answer to Anthropic's Claude Science workbench and similar productivity-layer products. Daily Brief represents a bet that persistent context across productivity apps — rather than standalone chat sessions — is the next battleground for AI assistants.
