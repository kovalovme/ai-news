## GPT-5.6 Sol: Cerebras deployment begins, public pricing disclosed <a id="gpt-56-sol-cerebras"></a>

**Source:** [OpenAI preview post](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/) · [AESOP AI News](https://aesopacademy.org/ai-news/articles/2026-07-01-openai-gpt56-sol-cerebras-750-tokens-per-second) · **Type:** update · **Time (UTC):** —

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, first previewed June 26 in a government-gated rollout of ~20 approved organizations, is now beginning its Cerebras wafer-scale inference deployment in July at up to 750 tokens per second — roughly 6–18× the 40–120 tok/s range typical for GPU-cluster serving of frontier-class models. Pricing has been publicly disclosed: $5 per million input tokens and $30 per million output tokens, with cached reads at a 90% discount. Access remains restricted while Cerebras scales capacity, but this represents the first expansion beyond the initial government-only cohort.

**Why it matters:** The Cerebras lane confirms that OpenAI is betting on wafer-scale inference as a competitive differentiator for latency-sensitive agentic workflows, not just raw throughput demos. The $5/$30 pricing sets a new reference ceiling for government-class frontier models; for comparison, Claude Sonnet 5 launched at $2/$10 on June 30.
