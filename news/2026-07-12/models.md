# Models — 2026-07-12

## Gemini 3.5 Pro — GA scheduled July 17 <a id="gemini-35-pro"></a>

**Source:** [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/) · **Type:** Upcoming release · **Time (UTC):** —

Google DeepMind has confirmed July 17 as the general availability date for Gemini 3.5 Pro, a model rebuilt from scratch rather than incrementally updated from Gemini 2.5. Key confirmed specs: a 2 million token context window (the largest in any production frontier model to date), a Deep Think reasoning layer for complex problem-solving, and autonomous workflow capabilities covering coding tasks, tool use, and execution with minimal human oversight. Pricing is expected to open around $1.25 per million input tokens, a significant undercut of Fable 5 ($10) and GPT-5.6 Sol ($5).

**Why it matters:** The 2M context window eliminates practically all retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workarounds for long-document tasks. If performance matches the architectural investment, Gemini 3.5 Pro will be the first model where cost, context, and frontier-tier reasoning converge in a single offering — a direct challenge to both Anthropic and OpenAI's enterprise positioning.
