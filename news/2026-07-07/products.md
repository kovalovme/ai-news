# Products — 2026-07-07

## Fable 5 subscription inclusion ends today <a id="fable-5-billing-cliff"></a>

**Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319767/20260706/fable-5-subscription-ends-tomorrow-per-token-costs-who-gets-hit-hardest.htm) · **Type:** update · **Time (UTC):** Jul 7

July 7 is the final day Fable 5 is bundled within Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise subscription plans at up to 50% of weekly usage allocation. Starting July 8, all Fable 5 access requires usage credits billed at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens—the highest per-token rate Anthropic has listed for a generally available model, and double the rate of Claude Opus 4.8.

Developers must explicitly enable the usage-credits billing mechanism in the Claude Console before the transition occurs; without credits enabled, Fable 5 access stops with no grace period. Teams that have not yet done so should complete the setup today.

| Model | Input | Output | Plan inclusion |
|-------|-------|--------|----------------|
| Fable 5 (from Jul 8) | $10/MTok | $50/MTok | Credits only |
| Claude Opus 4.8 | $5/MTok | $25/MTok | Subscription |
| Claude Sonnet 5 (intro to Aug 31) | $2/MTok | $10/MTok | Subscription |

**Why it matters:** The 5–10× pricing gap between Fable 5 and Sonnet 5 (currently at introductory rates) means teams need to explicitly justify Fable 5 use by task. High-value agentic tasks where Fable 5 capability is measurably superior may warrant the cost; routine generation workloads do not. This is also a notable moment economically: Fable 5 transitioning from "included" to "metered" signals Anthropic moving its most capable model out of the flat-rate tier.
