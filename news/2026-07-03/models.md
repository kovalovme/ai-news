# Models — 2026-07-03

## Meta "Watermelon" matches GPT-5.5 on internal benchmarks <a id="meta-watermelon"></a>

**Source:** [Business Insider via Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/260702/p40) · **Type:** update · **Time (UTC):** ~00:00

Meta Superintelligence Labs chief Alexandr Wang told employees at an internal town hall that the company's next flagship model, codenamed Watermelon, has reached GPT-5.5-level performance on "several" internal benchmarks. He also confirmed that Watermelon uses an order of magnitude more compute than its predecessor Avocado. The model is still in training and has no announced release date; the specific benchmarks used were not disclosed publicly.

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest signal yet that Meta is closing the capability gap with OpenAI's current public frontier after Avocado's mixed reception. At 10× the compute of Avocado, Watermelon will be among the most expensive models ever trained — a significant bet on scaling returns at a moment when several competitors are pursuing efficiency-first approaches.

## Gemini 3.5 Pro enters government pre-release framework talks <a id="gemini-35-pro-gov"></a>

**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-targets-loopholes-used-by-chinese-firms-to-access-claude-ft-reports-4774998) · **Type:** update · **Time (UTC):** —

Reuters reports that Google is in pre-release framework conversations with the US government ahead of the Gemini 3.5 Pro launch, with the model potentially triggering a "covered frontier model" designation under the June 2 executive order. The model remains in limited Vertex AI enterprise preview. Two high-profile departures add context: Noam Shazeer (Gemini co-lead, "Attention Is All You Need" co-author) left for OpenAI, and John Jumper (Google DeepMind VP, 2024 Nobel laureate for AlphaFold) joined Anthropic. No release date has been set.

**Why it matters:** Gemini 3.5 Pro's government-framework coordination, if confirmed, would make it the third frontier model (after Fable 5 and GPT-5.6) to pass through this new pre-release review channel — completing the pattern across all three major US labs. The departure of two senior Gemini contributors to rival labs adds engineering risk to an already-delayed launch.
