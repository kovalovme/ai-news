## China's AI Companion Regulation Takes Effect: Doubao and Qwen Agent Features Shut Down <a id="china-companion-regulation-live"></a>

**Source:** [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/07/06/bytedances-doubao-and-alibabas-qwen-to-shut-down-ai-agent-features-on-july-15/) · [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3359482/bytedance-and-alibaba-disable-humanlike-ai-custom-agents-new-rules-loom) · **Type:** regulation · **Time (UTC):** 00:00

China's "Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services" — co-issued in April 2026 by the Cyberspace Administration of China, NDRC, MIIT, Ministry of Public Security, and SAMR — took effect today. ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen have shut down all user-created AI agent features and humanlike interactive agent services. Doubao users retain read-only access to agent configurations and chat histories through October 15, 2026; after that date, all data will be processed per platform privacy policy and become unrecoverable. Alibaba has announced no equivalent grace period for Qwen agent data. ByteDance redirects displaced users to Maoxiang, a separate ByteDance app for agent creation.

The regulation requires services featuring persistent memory, relationship continuity, or humanlike persona to implement anti-addiction systems, mandatory usage notifications and instant-exit mechanisms, and real-time detection of "unhealthy dependence" — features structurally incompatible with agents designed to maintain coherent long-term user relationships. The regulation was previously anticipated in the July 11 digest.

**Why it matters:** This is the first major regulatory action to directly shut down a category of AI product at scale. The two affected platforms collectively serve hundreds of millions of users. The regulation creates a structural asymmetry: Chinese AI labs now face compliance costs for companion and persistent-agent products that their US and European competitors do not, potentially narrowing the range of AI products Chinese companies can operate domestically. Companies redirecting users to alternative apps also risk data fragmentation and churn.

## TSMC Posts All-Time Revenue Record on AI Chip Demand <a id="tsmc-q2-revenue-record"></a>

**Source:** [TechZine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/infrastructure/142833/tsmc-exceeds-q2-expectations-with-record-revenue/) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tsmc-q2-revenue-surges-36-amid-robust-ai-demand-4787449) · **Type:** business · **Time (UTC):** —

TSMC's Q2 2026 consolidated revenue reached T$1.27 trillion (US$39.62 billion), a 36% increase year-on-year and a new all-time quarterly record. June 2026 alone produced NT$442.68 billion — approximately US$13.8 billion — a 67.9% jump from June 2025 and the largest single-month revenue in the company's nearly four-decade history. TSMC reported that its advanced 3nm (N3) process node is sold out through the end of 2026, with demand driven primarily by Nvidia H200/H300 AI accelerator production and Apple A20-series SoCs. The company exceeded analyst consensus estimates for the quarter.

**Why it matters:** TSMC's revenue is one of the most reliable independent signals for aggregate AI compute spend, since it sits upstream of both hyperscaler training clusters and inference deployments. A 36% YoY quarterly gain and a +67.9% single-month spike confirm that the AI chip buildout being reported by Nvidia, AMD, and hyperscalers in their own earnings calls is translating to real wafer demand, not just order-book activity. N3 sold out through year-end also means no near-term relief for AI chip supply constraints.
